# Surface brief: Overview (app shell)

Mode: Operate. Build path: code-led.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Guardrails are the hero, not the balance. The category default puts a big number and a line chart on top and buries risk settings in Settings; this surface draws the user's rules on the chart itself as load lines, with headroom read beside them.
OWN-WORLD: Admiralty nautical chart. Deep chart-ink navy shell, pale sea-grey ground, white work surfaces, and chart magenta reserved for the user's limits and the agent's annotations (as charts reserve magenta for hazards). Teal and vermilion only for gain and loss. One workhorse sans (Geist), tabular numerals.
STORY: The user sees value, how far they are from each limit, and what the agent did about it; they ask "why" by tap or voice and hear the answer.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy rail left. Top bar: title, market clock, Talk button (primary). Left 2/3: value and day change over the chart, floor line and daily limit drawn in magenta, agent action markers on the line. Right 1/3: two guardrail headroom bars, editable in place. Below: agent activity with inline Why.
FORM: Hand-chosen (launcher not run), 1st of 7 on the list; seed: none (unavailable).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
